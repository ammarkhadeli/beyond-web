# Presentation screenshots

`iss-approach.png` and `moon-descent.png` are actual browser captures of the Beyond game by Batata Warriors. They show labeled practice starts, not completed mission playthroughs or photographs of real spacecraft.

The ISS image combines original training-craft geometry with credited station and Earth assets. The lunar image includes credited lunar-module geometry and lunar terrain/imagery. Source assets retain their separate rights and attributions in [the asset record](../assets/credits.json). Original code and procedural models received development-time AI assistance, as described in [the disclosure](../docs/ASSETS-AND-AI.md); these captures are rendered simulation output, not image-generator artwork.

No NASA endorsement, operational certification, hardware performance result, or complete-journey verification is implied by these images.
